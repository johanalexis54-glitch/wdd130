Bogota DC.

Bogotá is Colombia's sprawling, high-altitude capital. La Candelaria, its cobblestone center, boasts colonial sites such as the neoclassical Teatro Colón and the 17th-century Church of San Francisco. It also houses popular museums, including the Botero Museum, which displays art by Fernando Botero, and the Gold Museum, with its collection of pre-Columbian gold artifacts.
