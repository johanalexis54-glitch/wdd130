Our History

We were born amidst the majestic mountains of Santander, inspired by the wildness of the Fonce River and the adventurous spirit of San Gil. What began as a group of friends passionate about whitewater rafting has become Rafting Adventure, the leading river experience company in the region. With over 10 years of experience navigating the Santander canyons, we have transformed adrenaline into a safe art form, allowing thousands of people to discover the natural magic of Colombia from a unique perspective: the heart of the river.

Mission

To provide safe and unforgettable adventure experiences on the rivers of Santander, promoting respect for Colombian biodiversity and the development of local tourism in San Gil.

Vision

By 2030, to be recognized as the leading ecotourism and adventure sports company in Colombia, distinguished by our technical innovation and our commitment to the conservation of watersheds.
